Plain-language policy

Privacy

Effective 28 August 2026

Your planning data stays local

Resources, services, imported calendar events, and calculation history are stored in your browser’s IndexedDB. ICS files are parsed on your device. We do not upload or receive their contents, and there is no account or analytics tracker.

Backups and deletion

You can export your plan as JSON at any time. “Clear local plan” deletes the planner database from this browser. You can also remove site data using your browser settings.

Licenses and checkout

If you buy the field kit, checkout is hosted by Sociobot/Dodo, the merchant of record. This app stores the returned license token and its verification result in local storage and sends the token to the Sociobot verification endpoint at most once per day. Payment information is never handled by this app.

Network access

Except for an optional license verification request, the installed planner requires no service backend. Service-worker requests are only used to cache the app on your device.

Contact

For privacy questions, contact privacy@sociobot.in.
