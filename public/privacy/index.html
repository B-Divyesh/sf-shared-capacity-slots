Privacy policy

Privacy

Effective 6 September 2026

Your planning data stays local

Resources, services, imported calendar events, and change history are stored in your browser’s IndexedDB. ICS files are parsed on your device. We do not upload or receive their contents. The site has no accounts, analytics, or trackers.

Sample data stays separate

The demo runs in memory. It does not read or write your saved plan. Leaving or resetting the demo discards its changes.

Backups and deletion

You can export your plan as JSON. “Clear local plan” deletes the planner database from this browser. Browser settings can also remove all site data.

Licenses and checkout

If you buy the field kit, Sociobot/Dodo hosts checkout as the merchant of record. This app stores the returned license token and its verification result in local storage. It sends the token to the Sociobot verification endpoint at most once per day. This app never handles payment information.

Network access

Except for an optional license check, the installed planner needs no service backend. The service worker only requests this site to cache the app.

Privacy requests

Email privacy@sociobot.in with a privacy question. We cannot retrieve local plans because they never reach us.
